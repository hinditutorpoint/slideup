Built for Performance & Privacy

Everything you need to play, edit, stream, read, and protect your media in one lightweight app.

🎬

Ultra-HD Video Player

Powered by MediaKit (libmpv backend). Supports dual-side gesture controls for brightness/volume, native Picture-in-Picture, speed regulation (0.25x – 4.0x), and multi-audio track switching.

📺

IPTV & Live Streams

Add playlists via M3U URLs, local files, or XTream Codes API. Watch live in a split player with category browsing and 28+ language quick-start presets.

✂️

FFmpeg Video Editor

Trim, cut, crop, rotate, adjust speed, mix audio, and apply filters with hardware acceleration. Frame extraction and screenshot capture in lossless formats.

🔄

Batch Media Converter

Transcode audio and video across dozens of formats (MP4, MKV, MP3, FLAC, AAC, WebM). Foreground service ensures background batch conversions never stop.

📚

Document & E-Book Reader

Distraction-free viewer for PDF, EPUB, and TXT files. Continuous scroll, search, page bookmarks, font scaling, and night reading modes.

🔒

Biometric File Vault

Protect confidential videos, audio recordings, and documents behind biometric fingerprint, Face Unlock, or PIN authentication.

🌐

Private Incognito Browser

Ad-suppression, tracking protection, and direct media sniffing to download or play web videos and documents inside SlideUp.

🛡️

Zero Tracking & Offline First

No advertisements, no telemetry tracking, no account logins required. Your media stays 100% on your device.
