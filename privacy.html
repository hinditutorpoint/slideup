Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Inkpad IT Solutions ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Slideup Media Player mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By downloading, accessing, or using the App, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

🔒 Core Privacy Promise: Slideup Media Player is primarily a local multimedia player and reader. Your local media, videos, audio, images, and documents remain strictly on your device and are NEVER uploaded or sold to any external servers.

1. Information We Access & Collect

1.1 Device Storage & Local Files

The App requests read/manage access to local storage solely to discover, index, organize, and play your media files (Video, Audio, Images) and documents (PDF, EPUB, TXT). This indexing is performed strictly offline on your device.

1.2 Network & Online Content

When you stream IPTV channels, search public domain media from Archive.org, or browse web content in the private browser, standard network requests are routed directly to the respective host servers. We do not store or track your personal browsing history on remote servers.

1.3 Automatically Generated App Settings

App preferences such as volume levels, brightness, theme settings, bookmarks, and playlists are stored locally on your device using SQLite and Hive databases.

2. Permissions and Their Purposes

To provide high-quality playback capabilities, the App may request the following permissions:

  • Storage / Media Access: To scan and play local video, audio, and document files.
  • Foreground Service (Media Playback / Processing): To keep audio, streams, and video conversion tasks running seamlessly when the screen is locked or the app is minimized.
  • Post Notifications: To display interactive media playback controls (Play, Pause, Skip) and download progress in the system status bar.
  • Internet Access: To stream online IPTV playlists, query Archive.org public resources, and load user-requested websites.
  • Biometric / Fingerprint (Optional): Used exclusively to unlock local locked files when configured by the user. No biometric data is stored by the App.

3. Third-Party Services

3.1 Internet Archive (Archive.org)

The App allows searching public catalog resources from Archive.org. Accessing such content is subject to Archive.org's terms and privacy policies.

3.2 Embedded Web View

The in-app private browser allows users to visit third-party websites. Web pages load under their respective website operators' privacy terms. The private browser does not persist cookies, local storage, or session history to disk when private mode is active.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data with third-party advertisers or data brokers. We may only disclose information if required by law or in good faith belief that such action is necessary to comply with legal obligations.

5. Data Security

We utilize industry-standard practices, including HTTPS encryption for all external network requests, strict sandboxed local storage, and secure Android scoped storage compliance.

6. User Control & Data Deletion

You have full control over your data:

  • You can clear app cache, playback history, and saved playlists anytime via Settings > Clear Cache.
  • Uninstalling the App permanently deletes all locally cached app configurations and databases from your device.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
